Loose Leaf is a small PDF editor that works entirely on your phone. It has no network permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your original file is never changed: saving always writes a new copy.

What it does:

* Open PDFs from the file picker, "Open with" or "Share"
* Highlight, draw with a pen, add lines, arrows, rectangles and ovals, cover things with white-out
* Add text boxes: size, colour, move, resize
* Stamp check marks, crosses and today's date onto forms that cannot be filled in directly
* Draw or type a signature once and place it wherever you need it
* Fill in PDF forms
* Redact: black boxes that really remove what they cover from the saved copy
* Find text and jump to a page
* Reorder, rotate, delete and extract pages; merge another PDF in
* Make a smaller copy with a choice of quality levels
* Save a copy protected with a password
* Optionally keep everything you save, or just what you signed, in a library folder that you can organise in the app
* Make scanned pages searchable with offline text recognition (English included; other languages can be added from a file)

If the app is closed before you saved, your edits are still there the next time you open it.

Every feature is a module that can be switched off in Settings, which removes its buttons and menu entries.
